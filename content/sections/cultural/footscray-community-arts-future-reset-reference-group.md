The Future Reset project: Diasporic Dreaming is a community-led initiative that seeks to support young people from African Diaspora communities through contemporary art outcomes.

I was commissioned as a consultant alongside a group of other African artists, leveraging our diverse art practices to help design programs for the Future Reset Project.
