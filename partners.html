Santuri East Africa is a community-focussed music innovation hub based in Nairobi, Kenya. We are the home of the Santuri Electronic Music Academy (SEMA), where you can learn music production and DJing, and Kilele Summit — the annual conference and festival celebrating adventurous music and culture.
