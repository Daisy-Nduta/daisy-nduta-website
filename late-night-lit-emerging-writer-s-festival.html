Late Night Lit - Emerging Writer's Festival

Presented a commissioned sound work as part of Late Night Lit: Yesterday: A Live Listening Party, an exhibition bringing together audio artists from across Australia. The event explored memory and the traces of everyday and monumental moments through sound, bringing five artists together to transform stories, experiences and yesterdays into immersive sonic works.
