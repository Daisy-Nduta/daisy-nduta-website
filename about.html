About Frequency Shift

Making room for a different signal.

Frequency Shift is an online radio platform and gathering point for communities in and beyond Nairobi. It makes room for shared listening, new voices, and the conversations that emerge when sound moves between people, places, and disciplines.

Alongside radio, Frequency Shift develops art installations, live-streamed events, and performances that bring artists and audiences into live exchange. Its work explores broadcast as a living space: collective, responsive, and open to experiment.
