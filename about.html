About Daisy

Hi, I'm Daisy.

Nairobi-based, sound-first, and just as at home behind a mic on a film set as I am producing a night that pulls a room together. Here's a closer look at the work.

Daisy Nduta is a multidisciplinary creative and cultural producer working across sound, live events, and community-led arts. Her practice is rooted in the craft of sound — she trained as a sound engineer, and that foundation still shapes how she approaches every project, whether the work is heard through headphones or felt across a crowd.

Her work spans Sound — audio post-production and broadcast; Film — location recording and sound design for narrative and documentary storytelling; Curation & Production — event producing and cultural programming; and Cultural Projects — longer-term, multidisciplinary, community-rooted initiatives — including credits at the Melbourne International Film Festival, Golden State Film Festival, and Melbourne Fringe Festival, and events for Melbourne Music Week and MPavilion.

Based in Nairobi. Working across East Africa, Australia, and beyond.
