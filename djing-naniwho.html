Daisy the Artist

naniwho

naniwho, is a genre-fluid DJ and multidisciplinary creative whose sets draw from years of cross-cultural listening shaped by living between Nairobi and Melbourne.

Since beginning her DJ journey in June 2025, she has quickly built momentum, playing notable events and venues across Nairobi bringing a thoughtful yet dynamic energy to each set.

Her sound moves between soulful, percussive, and unexpected blends, rooted in diasporic rhythms and global influences. With a background in sound engineering and radio naniwho has developed a wide sonic palette and a strong instinct for curation. Her mixes center memory, movement, and connection, always inviting listeners into an immersive and evolving journey.

Currently based in Nairobi and open to working across borders.
